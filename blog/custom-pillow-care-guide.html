How to Wash and Store Custom Photo Pillows

By Emma Thompson • September 4, 2026 • 7 min read

Short answer: custom photo pillow covers from $24.99 are machine washable once the insert comes out, and cold washes with a slow spin keep the print sharp.

Bottom line: store pillows upright in a breathable cotton bag, never vacuum-sealed, and rotate them a quarter turn weekly so one face does not take all the pressure.

A photo pillow lives a harder life than a blanket. It gets leaned on, dropped on the floor, and sat on by guests who do not know it is personal. After styling custom pillows in staged living rooms for three years, I have settled on a care routine that keeps both the print and the shape intact through a full season of use.

Washing the Cover

Every cover we tested unzips fully, which is the whole point of the design. Empty the insert, close the zip, and wash the cover cold on a gentle cycle inside a mesh laundry bag. The slow spin matters more than people expect: a fast spin presses deep creases into the print that take days to relax. Skip fabric softener, which coats the fibres and dulls the photo slightly. Line dry flat or tumble on no heat; a hot dryer can wave the edges of the print panel.

Caring for the Insert

The inserts themselves should not be washed unless the label says so - most are polyester fibre fill that clumps when soaked. Instead, air them out twice a year in fresh air and sunlight for a few hours, which deals with most odours. If a spill soaks through to the insert, spot clean with a damp cloth and a drop of detergent, then let it dry fully before it goes back in the cover, or the dampness will mark the print from the inside.

Storage and Daily Habits

For storage between seasons, a breathable cotton bag on a shelf beats a vacuum bag every time. Vacuum compression sets permanent creases into the print and flattens the fill so badly it may never fully recover. In daily use, rotate the pillow a quarter turn each week and plump it from the edges inward after use. A pillow that always leans the same way develops a permanent lean and a faded stripe where the sun hits it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you wash a custom photo pillow in the machine?

The cover, yes: unzip it, remove the insert, and wash cold on a gentle cycle inside a mesh bag. The insert itself should only be spot cleaned unless its label says machine washable.

Why did the print on my pillow cover crease?

A fast spin cycle presses creases into the print while the fabric is wet and soft. Use a slow spin and dry the cover flat; the creases relax as the fibres settle.

How do I fix a flattened pillow insert?

Plump it from the edges inward daily for a week and air it in sunlight for a few hours. Mildly flattened fill usually recovers. Fill that has been vacuum-compressed for months often will not.

Is it safe to iron a photo pillow cover?

Iron only the reverse side of the cover, never over the print, and use a low setting with a pressing cloth. Direct heat on the printed panel can gloss or mark the image.
