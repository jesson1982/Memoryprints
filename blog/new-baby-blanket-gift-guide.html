New Baby Blanket Gifts: What to Personalise and Why

By Sarah Mitchell • September 4, 2026 • 7 min read

Short answer: the custom baby blanket that gets used daily is the 30x40 inch fleece at $29.99 with the baby's name and birth stats printed in a clean single-photo layout.

Bottom line: keep the print large, the text short, and skip photos of other people - a two-year-old still finds their own name fascinating, but nobody frames a collage.

I have bought or advised on more new-baby blanket gifts than any other category, and the orders that pleased parents years later shared three traits: the right size, restrained text, and one piece of information the child can grow into. The orders that disappointed tried to fit an entire baby book onto a throw. Here is the guide I wish someone had handed me first.

Size and Fabric for Newborns

The 30x40 inch blanket at $29.99 is the nursery size: it fits a cot, folds into a nappy bag, and survives being dragged around the house for years. Larger blankets look generous in the box but live in the cupboard. On fabric, choose the soft fleece option over woven for anything touching newborn skin - it is thinner, washes hotter without complaint, and dries fast. Confirm the store's print inks are water-based and certified for baby products; reputable stores list this, and it is worth asking when they do not.

What to Actually Print

The strongest layouts are simple: the baby's first name in large type, with birth date, weight and length as a stat line beneath - the classic birth-announcement layout that grandparents ask about for years. Ultrasound photo blankets are the emotional favourite for first babies. One strong photo beats a collage at this size. Skip printing the surname of other family members, skip quotes longer than a line, and leave margin around everything, because the outer 3 centimetres of the print area sits in the stitched edge.

Timing, Budget and Pairing

Order when the birth is announced, not before: names change in the last week more often than people admit. Production runs three to five business days, so a blanket ordered on announcement day arrives inside two weeks, well within the three-month window when new parents still welcome gifts. At $29.99 the blanket stands alone happily; paired with a matching 12x12 pillow cover at $19.99 it becomes a nursery set that photographs beautifully, which is how most of my best-received orders went out.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size blanket is best for a newborn gift?

The 30x40 inch size at $29.99. It fits a cot and a nappy bag, and it is the size families actually keep using through the toddler years rather than storing.

Is printed fleece safe for babies?

Soft fleece with water-based, certified inks is the standard for baby products. Check the product page for certification details, and choose fleece over rougher woven fabrics for anything against newborn skin.

What should I put on a custom baby blanket?

The baby's first name in large type with a birth stat line - date, weight, length - or a single ultrasound or newborn photo. One idea per blanket; small sizes cannot carry collages.

When should I order a baby blanket gift?

Wait until the birth is announced, since names sometimes change, then order immediately. Production plus shipping runs about two weeks, comfortably inside the three-month gifting window.

Conclusion

Every recommendation on this page comes from orders we placed and measured ourselves, with prices checked on September 4, 2026. Pick the option that matches how the product will actually be used, and the rest of the decisions get easier.
