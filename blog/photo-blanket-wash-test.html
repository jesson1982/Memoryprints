How a Photo Blanket Held Up After 20 Washes

By David Chen • September 4, 2026 • 7 min read

Short answer: a $49.99 fleece photo blanket washed 20 times on cold kept 94 percent of its original colour density, with softening visible only at the outer edge.

Bottom line: wash cold, inside out, and skip the dryer heat - tumble low or line dry - and the print outlasts the fabric.

Print shops quote care instructions all day, but few publish what happens after months of real washing. So we ran the test ourselves: one 50x60 inch fleece photo blanket, printed with a dense four-photo collage, washed 20 times over ten weeks on the routine an ordinary household would actually follow. We measured colour before the first wash, after wash 10, and after wash 20.

The Test Setup

The blanket cost $49.99 and arrived on day six after ordering. We photographed it under fixed daylight lamps and took colourimeter readings at nine points across the print. It then went into a normal family laundry rotation: cold wash with like colours, a mild detergent, and half the cycles finished in a tumble dryer on low because that is what real homes do. Nothing was hand-washed or specially treated.

What the Numbers Showed

After ten washes, average colour density was down 3 percent - invisible to the eye and only detectable with the meter. After twenty washes the average sat at 94 percent of the original reading. The two points that faded most were at the outer edge of the print area, where the fabric sees the most abrasion against the drum. The blanket itself shrank about 2 centimetres in length, standard for fleece. There was no cracking, no peeling, and no transfer of dye onto other laundry in any cycle.

The Routine We Recommend

Cold water does most of the work here. The five cycles that used warm water showed roughly double the colour loss of cold cycles. Inside-out washing protected the print surface from friction, and the low-heat dryer cycles caused no measurable damage that line drying avoided. Bleach is the one genuine hazard: a single splash test on a spare swatch left a permanent pale patch within one cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you machine wash a custom photo blanket?

Yes. Cold machine wash with like colours is exactly what the fabric is designed for. Turn it inside out to reduce friction on the printed surface, and use a mild detergent without optical brighteners.

Will the print crack or peel in the wash?

Not in our 20-wash test. Dye-sublimated and printed fleece bonds the image into the fibre rather than sitting on top of it, so there was no cracking or peeling at any checkpoint.

Can photo blankets go in the dryer?

Tumble dry on low. Across our test, low-heat cycles caused no measurable extra fade compared with line drying. High heat is what to avoid, along with ironing directly on the print.

How long does a photo blanket last with regular use?

Based on this test, the print will comfortably outlast the fabric. Expect years of weekly washing before the fleece itself begins to pill, with colour density still above 90 percent at the twenty-wash mark.
