Custom Blanket Sizes and Prices: A Buyer's Guide

By Sarah Mitchell • September 4, 2026 • 7 min read

Short answer: the three standard photo blanket sizes cover nearly every gift: 30x40 at $29.99 for babies and pets, 50x60 at $49.99 for one or two photos on a sofa, and 60x80 at $69.99 for wedding collages.

Bottom line: when choosing between sizes, count the photos first - more than four images needs the 60x80, and anything under three works better on the 30x40.

Size is the decision that changes everything else about a custom blanket order: the price, the shipping cost, and whether the photo layout works at all. After ordering blankets for weddings, babies, graduations and memorials over five years, I now pick the size before I pick the photos. This guide lays out what each size costs, what it physically fits, and the mistakes I made before I learned that order.

The Three Sizes and What They Fit

The 30x40 inch blanket at $29.99 is a lap and crib size. It holds one strong photo, or a name-and-date layout, and it is the right choice for a new baby, a pet portrait, or a throw for a reading chair. The 50x60 inch at $49.99 is the sofa workhorse and the best seller for good reason: it comfortably fits three or four photos plus text. The 60x80 inch at $69.99 is the bed and collage size, handling six to eight photos in a grid without anything looking cramped.

Where the Money Actually Goes

Postage scales with size more than people expect. In my orders, shipping a single 30x40 ran $8.90 while the 60x80 ran $14.50, and two 50x60 blankets in one box cost barely more than one shipped alone. If your budget is fixed near $60, a 50x60 blanket plus a small add-on like a matching pillow cover usually lands a better gift than one 60x80 blanket with nothing beside it.

Choosing by Occasion

Weddings and anniversaries justify the 60x80 because collage layouts of the day look best large. Graduations sit happily on a 50x60 with one portrait, the school name and the year. Memorials are the one case where I push people down a size rather than up: a 30x40 or 50x60 with a single portrait reads as a keepsake, while an oversized collage can feel busy in a grief context.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size custom blanket is most popular?

The 50x60 inch size at $49.99. It fits a sofa drape, holds three to five photos comfortably, and ships at a mid-range cost, which makes it the safest default when you are unsure.

How many photos fit on a 60x80 blanket?

Six to eight photos in a grid layout, or three to four large photos with room for names and dates. Beyond eight images the individual photos start losing detail at print resolution.

Is a bigger blanket worth the extra cost?

Only if the photo count justifies it. A 60x80 with two photos looks sparse; the same two photos on a 50x60 look intentional. Choose size after counting photos.

Do blanket sizes include border space?

Yes - the printed area is slightly smaller than the blanket itself to allow for edge stitching. Keep key faces away from the outer 3 centimetres of your layout.

Conclusion

Every recommendation on this page comes from orders we placed and measured ourselves, with prices checked on September 4, 2026. Pick the option that matches how the product will actually be used, and the rest of the decisions get easier.
