Returns Policy

Short answer: Personalised items are remade or refunded whenever there is a print defect or transit damage.

Bottom line: Photograph the issue within 14 days of delivery and we remake or refund; the steps are below.

Our Return Policy

At MemoryPrints, we want you to be completely satisfied with your custom products. Because each item is personalized just for you, our return policy differs from standard retail returns.

Custom Items

  • Personalized Items: Due to the custom nature of personalized products, we cannot accept returns or exchanges unless there is a manufacturing defect or error on our part.
  • Manufacturing Errors: If your item has a printing error, wrong image, or quality issue, please contact us within 30 days of delivery for a free replacement or refund.
  • Design Approval: Please review your design carefully before placing your order. We are not responsible for design errors approved by customers.

Non-Personalized Items

  • Standard Returns: Non-personalized items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange.
  • Condition: Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.
  • Return Shipping: Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to a manufacturing error.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our customer service team at support@memoryprints.com
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Include photos of any defects or issues (if applicable)
  4. Receive return authorization and shipping instructions
  5. Ship the item back using the provided return label

Refund Process

  • Processing Time: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving your returned item.
  • Refund Method: Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for purchase.
  • Notification: You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed.

Exchanges

Exchanges are available for non-personalized items in different sizes or colors. For personalized items with errors, we will provide a free replacement with the corrected design.

Cancellations

  • Within 24 Hours: Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement for a full refund.
  • After 24 Hours: Once production has begun, orders cannot be cancelled but may be eligible for return under our standard policy.
  • Rush Orders: Rush orders cannot be cancelled once placed due to expedited processing.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

  • Damaged Items: If your item arrives damaged, contact us immediately with photos for a free replacement.
  • Lost Packages: If your package is lost in transit, we will file a claim with the carrier and either reship your order or provide a refund.
  • Wrong Address: We are not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by customers.

Contact Information

For any questions about our returns policy or to initiate a return, please contact:

  • Phone: 1-800-MEMORY

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to report a problem?

You have 14 days from the delivery date to report a print defect, transit damage, or a wrong design. After 14 days we review cases individually, so contact us as soon as you spot an issue.

Can I return a personalised blanket if I changed my mind?

Because every item is printed to order, we cannot restock or resell it. Unused items in original packaging can be swapped for store credit within 14 days, but made-to-order products are not eligible for a cash refund unless they are faulty.

Who pays the return shipping?

We do, in every defect case. We email a prepaid label or arrange a courier pickup, and the remake ships as soon as the photos are approved - you do not need to wait for the original to reach us.
