Quick Design Tips

🎨 Use High-Resolution Images

Always use images with 300 DPI or higher for the best print quality. Low-resolution images may appear pixelated when printed.

📐 Consider Print Area

Design within the specified print area for each product. Avoid placing important elements too close to edges.

🌈 Check Color Contrast

Ensure good contrast between your design and the product base color for maximum visibility and impact.

👁️ Preview Before Ordering

Always use our preview tool to review your design from multiple angles before placing your order.
