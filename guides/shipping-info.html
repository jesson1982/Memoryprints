Shipping Information

Short answer: Standard production is 2-3 business days, with shipping adding 3-7 days depending on destination.

Bottom line: Order event items at least two weeks ahead; express options are shown at checkout.

Production Times

  • Standard Production: 2-3 business days
  • Rush Production: 1 business day (additional fee applies)
  • Large Orders: 3-5 business days for orders over 50 items

Shipping Options

Shipping Method Delivery Time Price
Standard Shipping 3-7 business days $5.99
Express Shipping 2-3 business days $12.99
Priority Shipping 1-2 business days $19.99
Free Shipping 5-10 business days Free (orders over $75)

International Shipping

  • Canada: 7-14 business days ($15.99)
  • UK/Europe: 10-15 business days ($24.99)
  • Australia: 12-18 business days ($29.99)
  • Other International: 14-21 business days (varies by location)

Order Tracking

All orders include tracking information sent via email once your order ships. You can track your package using the provided tracking number on our carrier's website.

Shipping Address

We ship to the address provided during checkout. Please ensure your shipping address is correct before placing your order. We are not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by customers.

PO Boxes and APO/FPO

We can ship to PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses via Standard Shipping. Express and Priority shipping options are not available for these addresses.

Multiple Shipments

For orders containing multiple items, we may ship items separately if they become available at different times. You will not be charged additional shipping fees for split shipments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does my order take to arrive?

Production takes 2-3 business days and standard shipping adds 3-7 business days depending on your location. Most customers receive blankets and pillows within 7-10 days of ordering.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, and most of Europe. International delivery adds 5-10 business days, and any customs duties are calculated at checkout where possible.

Can I track my order?

Every order ships with tracking. You receive a tracking number by email the moment the parcel leaves our facility, and the link works with your local carrier's site.
