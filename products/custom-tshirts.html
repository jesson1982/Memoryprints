Design Tips:

For best results, use high-resolution images (300 DPI). Vector files work best for logos and text. Consider the shirt color when designing - light colors work best on dark shirts and vice versa. Our design tool includes templates and guidelines to help you create the perfect design.

Care Instructions:

Machine wash cold with like colors, tumble dry low. Do not bleach. Do not dry clean. Iron inside out if needed. Following these care instructions will help maintain the print quality and shirt fit for years to come.
