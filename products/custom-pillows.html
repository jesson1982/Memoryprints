Design Tips:

For best results, use high-resolution photos (300 DPI or higher). Consider using collages for smaller pillows to include multiple memories. Our design tool allows you to add text, filters, and creative elements to make your pillow truly unique.

Care Instructions:

Spot clean with mild detergent and water. For deeper cleaning, dry clean only. Avoid machine washing to preserve the print quality and pillow shape. Air dry away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.
